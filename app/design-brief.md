# ANA INTERNATIONAL GROUP LLC / transport website design brief
Audience: freight brokers and shipping operations teams needing a clear path to discuss a load.
Design read: high-confidence, useful B2B information with no unverifiable promises.
Concept spine: "The road ahead" — highway horizon perspective becomes a navigation and content rhythm.
Delivery tier: editorial with bespoke truck imagery and quiet transition effects.
Animation mode: non-animated — user specifically asked for subtle entrance animations, fast mobile experience, and reduced-motion support.
Palette: user-specified deep navy #101D32, #FFFFFF, light gray #F3F5F7, orange #F47B20; color exception to palette bans comes from user brief.
Typography: strong geometric sans, system-based Arial/Helvetica with compressed headers, accessible readable body.
Homepage composition: fixed-width content grid, monumental truck-hero, asymmetrical About split, quiet freight-detail editorial panels, three-step arrangement, contact form and dark footer.
Hero art: photoreal US highway and illustrative truck, purposefully reserved text area left; dark navy scrim.
Section art: second truck image for About / equipment readiness; orange route-line motif.
Services: verified public general freight and interstate for-hire property carrier status; equipment unverified and no fleet size or performance metrics.
Contact info: show official FMCSA phone, address, USDOT and MC; one directory-listed email with owner verification pending.
Form: clear field labels, inline errors, native constraints and server validation, genuine D1 persistence, success state ONLY after 201.
CTAs: Request a Quote (nav orange, scroll), Contact Our Team (hero white outline, scroll), Submit Freight Inquiry (form dark).
Assets: photo #1 hero, photo #2 on informational panel, photo #3 brand OG cover with overlaid typography; CSS logo/roadline icon.
Responsiveness: mobile menu toggle, single-column conversion form, aspect-ratio imagery, sticky header, focus visible, reduced-motion alternatives.
Metadata: separate favicon, branded share image, title/description, robots and sitemap.

Address, phone and MC verified in FMCSA public registry; contact email comes from secondary published directory and should be owner-confirmed. General freight and utilities reported, no specific equipment published. Broker/shipper reviews: none found on CarrierSource as of October 2026, so do not fabricate testimonials.
